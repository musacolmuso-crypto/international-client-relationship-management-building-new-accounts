# Strategic Partnerships & Service Innovation

**Focus:** Key account management · Customer loyalty · Partnership development · Service innovation  
**Status:** Strategic proposal / initiative; verify what has been launched.

## Opportunity
A client relationship can be strengthened by looking beyond routine service delivery to the broader customer journey, relevant ecosystem partners and useful value-added services.

## Strategic idea
Explore partnerships with French tourism-related entities and customer outreach initiatives that support interest in France, in connection with visa, alongside document pre-check services intended to make application preparation clearer and more efficient.

## Account-management lens
1. **Understand stakeholder objectives:** Identify what institutional partners, applicants and relevant tourism stakeholders value.
2. **Map the ecosystem:** Identify potential partners and mutually useful collaboration opportunities.
3. **Shape the value proposition:** Explain how educational outreach and document readiness can reduce friction and support better customer experiences.
4. **Coordinate delivery:** Align commercial, operational, compliance and technology stakeholders.
5. **Measure value:** Track approved partnerships, service adoption, customer feedback and appropriate commercial indicators.
